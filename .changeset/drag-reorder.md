Drag to reorder in `array`, `blocks` and `references` fields. A grip on each row replaces the up and down arrows: drag it with a mouse, finger or pen, or focus it and use ↑ ↓ Home End (a screen reader hears the new position). An open row stays open while it moves, and a row's validation errors move with it (also when a row is removed). No change to stored data or the API. The "Move up" and "Move down" buttons are gone from the admin. See [Reordering lists](https://mnml-cms.vercel.app/fields#reordering-lists).
